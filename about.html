Who We Are

About AIF

A student organization at the University of Minnesota dedicated to exploring the future of AI in financial services.

Mission

Educating the Next Generation of AI-Driven Finance Leaders

AI in Finance (AIF) exists to bridge the gap between cutting-edge artificial intelligence and the rapidly evolving world of finance. We believe that students who understand both domains will be the leaders of tomorrow's financial industry.

Our organization provides a collaborative environment where curiosity meets ambition — whether you're a finance student exploring machine learning for the first time, or a CS student looking to apply your skills to markets and investment.

Activities

What We Do

AIF brings together education, community, and professional development throughout the academic year.

Educational Sessions

Regular meetings featuring presentations, case studies, and discussions on AI applications in trading, risk management, and financial planning.

Hands-On Workshops

Technical workshops covering tools like Python, machine learning libraries, financial data APIs, and large language models applied to finance.

Guest Speakers

Industry professionals and academics share their expertise on emerging trends, career advice, and real-world AI use cases in financial services.

Networking Events

Structured and casual opportunities to connect with peers, alumni, and employers who are actively hiring at the intersection of AI and finance.

Career Development

Resume reviews, interview prep, and recruiting insight for roles in fintech, investment banking, quant finance, and data science.

Research & Discussion

Facilitated conversations and independent projects exploring the ethics, economics, and mechanics of AI adoption across the financial industry.

Membership

Open to All UMN Students

Membership in AIF is open to all currently enrolled University of Minnesota students. We do not discriminate on the basis of major, year, background, or prior experience with AI or finance.

All active members are eligible to vote in elections, participate in events, and run for officer positions during annual spring elections.
