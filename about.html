WHO WE ARE

About AIF.

AIF helps students become highly capable AI users, then gives them real opportunities to apply those skills through projects with nonprofits across Minneapolis.

Mission

Fill the gap between classroom learning and real-world AI ability.

Most students spend years learning concepts, formulas, and theory, but get far fewer opportunities to apply those ideas in real work. At the same time, AI is becoming one of the most important tools students will use after graduation, yet structured AI education is still almost nonexistent.

AIF exists to close that gap. We help students become highly capable AI users, then apply those skills through practical workshops, speaker sessions, and real projects with nonprofits across Minneapolis.

Our beliefs

Students should graduate with more than classroom knowledge.

We believe education should prepare students for the way work actually happens. That means learning how to think, solve problems, use modern tools, and create value outside of a textbook or exam.

AI is not a side topic anymore. It is becoming part of finance, business, research, operations, marketing, analytics, and almost every professional field. Students should not be left to figure it out on their own. They should have a place to practice, experiment, build judgment, and learn how to use AI responsibly and effectively.

That is what AIF is built for.

IN PRACTICE

How that plays out.

LEARN THE TOOLS

Workshops focus on practical AI skills students can use right away — prompting, managing context, developing ideas, improving outputs, and building better finance and business workflows.

HEAR FROM INDUSTRY

Guest speakers from finance, tech, and business show how AI is being used in real roles, teams, and decision-making processes.

BUILD THROUGH PROJECTS

Members apply what they learn through student-led projects, including work with nonprofits and organizations that can benefit from better analysis, reporting, and operations.

USE AI RESPONSIBLY

We treat AI as a powerful tool, not a shortcut. AIF emphasizes accuracy, ethics, judgment, and understanding the limits and tradeoffs behind the technology.
